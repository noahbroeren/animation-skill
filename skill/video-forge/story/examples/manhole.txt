# title: Why Square Manhole Covers Don't Work
# style: toonworld-explainer
# topic: why manhole covers are round (geometry: constant width)
In 1890, a mayor ordered every manhole cover in town made perfectly *square*.
He wanted modern streets, and square covers match the square tiles.
[place:foundry] The old foundry, the only one that casts covers, laughs at him.
@founder: Square? Good luck with that.
[place:street] But square covers are cheaper, and they stack neatly on a cart.
So by spring, half the town has square holes.
Then one morning, a worker lifts a cover, turns it to set it down, and it slips.
Straight down the hole.
Wait, what? The cover is bigger than the hole.
[beat:pivot] You see, a square is wider from corner to corner than from side to side: about *1.41* times.
Turn it on its diagonal, and it slides right through.
A circle is the same width whichever way you turn it, so it can never fall into its own hole.
By summer, the town quietly swaps every cover back.
[place:foundry] And the old foundry? They *doubled* their prices.
