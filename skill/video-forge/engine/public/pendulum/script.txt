A pendulum clock keeps time with nothing but gravity. And here's the strange part: how heavy the weight is doesn't matter at all. Only the length does. A one metre pendulum takes about two seconds to swing there and back. Make it four times longer, and it takes twice as long. That's why tall grandfather clocks have long pendulums. And on the Moon, where gravity is six times weaker, the same pendulum would swing almost two and a half times slower.
