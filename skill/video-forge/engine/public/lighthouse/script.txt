Lighthouses don't just blink. Each one blinks its own name. At night, a sailor sees nothing but darkness, and one tiny light. But which light? You see, every lighthouse on a coast gets its own pattern. Two quick flashes. A pause. One long flash. Sailors look that pattern up in a book called the List of Lights, and they know exactly where they are. So that light isn't just saying danger. It's saying: I'm here.
